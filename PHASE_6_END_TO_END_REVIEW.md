# Phase 6 — End-to-End Validation & Hackathon Demo Hardening

## A. Objective

The objective of Phase 6 is to perform rigorous end-to-end empirical validation and hackathon demo hardening of the Consumer Fraud Complaint Assistant. This phase proves that the complete system executes flawlessly across the entire victim lifecycle:

$$\text{VICTIM STORY} \rightarrow \text{ZERO-SHOT UNDERSTANDING} \rightarrow \text{STRUCTURED CASE} \rightarrow \text{MISSING INFO} \rightarrow \text{EVIDENCE MAP} \rightarrow \text{READINESS} \rightarrow \text{COMPLAINT GENERATION} \rightarrow \text{INDIAN ROUTING}$$

## E. Complaint Structure Validation

All generated formal complaints were validated against the mandatory 11-section statutory document structure:

1. `1. Subject` — Formal complaint title with classified category.
2. `2. Complainant Information` — Complainant identity header (`On file`).
3. `3. Incident Summary` — Raw victim statement preserved verbatim.
4. `4. Fraudulent Activity` — Zero-shot classification summary & confidence meter.
5. `5. Financial Loss` — Itemized loss breakdown (Amount, Currency, Method, Date, UTR).
6. `6. Suspect/Service Information` — Perpetrator handles, channels, and promised service.
7. `7. Communication Details` — Platform channels and personal data requested.
8. `8. Evidence Available` — Verifiable documentary & screenshot evidence map.
9. `9. Missing/Unavailable Information` — Explicit checklist of missing case facts.
10. `10. Requested Assistance` — Neutral, objective request for authority review.
11. `11. Declaration` — Formal truth declaration statement.

**Structure Compliance Rate**: `100% (11 / 11 Sections Verified across all cases)`

## F. Routing Validation

- **Fact-Based Evaluation**: Routing consumes the Phase 2 structured case record without running a second classifier.
- **Verified Link Integrity**: Uses only verified official government and payment authority links (`https://cybercrime.gov.in/`, `https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/`, `https://www.npci.org.in/`). Unofficial or third-party links are never generated.
- **Benign Routing Prevention**: Benign non-fraud inputs cleanly trigger `BENIGN_NO_ROUTING` without forcing false fraud pathways.

## I. Remaining System Limitations

1. **Client-Side Model Weights**: Model inference uses `Xenova/nli-deberta-v3-small` in WebAssembly (ONNX) format via Transformers.js. Initial load downloads ONNX weights (~40MB) into browser cache.
2. **Informational Guidance Only**: Routing recommendations are informational guidance based on user input and do not constitute formal legal counsel.
3. **No External Portal Submission**: The system generates formal pre-formatted complaint documents but does not automatically submit complaints to government APIs.
4. **Pretrained Language Model Knowledge**: Generalization relies on the semantic representations of pretrained Transformers; domain performance may vary on highly obscure regional dialects.
